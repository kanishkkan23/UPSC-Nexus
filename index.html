About UPSC Civil Services Examination

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Civil Services Examination (CSE) annually to recruit candidates for prestigious services including the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), Indian Foreign Service (IFS), and several other Central Government services.

Three Phases of UPSC Examination

Preliminary Examination

Objective-type examination consisting of General Studies Paper-I and CSAT.

Main Examination

Written examination designed to assess analytical ability and subject knowledge.

Personality Test

Final stage that evaluates personality, communication and decision-making skills.
