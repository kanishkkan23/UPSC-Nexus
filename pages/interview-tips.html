Personality Test Strategy

Presenting Your Best Self to the UPSC Board

The Personality Test is not a test of knowledge, but a test of character, integrity, intellectual capability, and balance of judgment. Learn how to master mock reviews and present answers with poise.

DAF Preparation Interview Etiquette

Decoding the Detailed Application Form (DAF)

Around 80% of the questions in the UPSC interview originate from your DAF. Thoroughly study and prepare every word written in your application:

  • Name and Surname — Understand the meaning, historical references, and famous personalities sharing your name.
  • Birthplace & State — Research demographic stats, geographic challenges, local history, administration problems, and unique cultural points.
  • Educational Background — Prepare fundamental core concepts of your graduation subject and its application to civil administration.
  • Hobbies & Achievements — Do not write fake hobbies. Research current developments, technical details, and famous achievements relating to your hobbies.

UPSC Board Etiquette & Tips

Your non-verbal communication, posture, and facial expressions speak volumes before you answer. Master these habits:

  • Postures and Greetings — Enter with permission, walk confidently, sit straight, place hands on your lap, make gentle eye contact, and politely greet the chairperson and other board members.
  • Handling Unknown Answers — Never guess or blabber. If you don't know the answer, confidently state: "I do not know the exact details, sir/ma'am. I will read up on this." Honesty is highly valued.
  • Balance of Judgment — Never take extreme ideological stances. Show progressive, balanced, constitutionally aligned, and problem-solving perspectives.
