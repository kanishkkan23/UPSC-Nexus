Essential NCERTs (Class VI - XII)

Before reading standard references, build your foundation with these NCERTs:

  • Polity — Class XI (Indian Constitution at Work), Class XII (Politics in India Since Independence).
  • History — Class XI (Themes in Indian History Parts 1-3), Class XII (Modern Themes).
  • Geography — Class XI (Fundamentals of Physical Geography & India: Physical Environment), Class XII (India: People and Economy).
  • Economics — Class XI (Indian Economic Development), Class XII (Introductory Macroeconomics).
  • Art & Culture — Class XI (An Introduction to Indian Art).
