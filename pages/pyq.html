Effective Strategy for Solving PYQs

Previous Year Questions help you understand the UPSC examination pattern, identify recurring themes, and improve answer quality. Follow these steps for maximum benefit.

  1. Read the entire question carefully before answering.
  2. Identify important keywords and concepts.
  3. Attempt the paper within the prescribed time limit.
  4. Analyze mistakes after completing each paper.
  5. Revise weak topics before attempting the next paper.
  6. Maintain a notebook of frequently repeated concepts.
