Mains Written Strategy

Structured Answers, Analytical Insights, and High Scores

The Mains stage evaluates your intellectual depth, critical understanding, and expression. Learn how to draft answers that stand out, cover General Studies (GS1-4), and write high-scoring essays.

Answer Writing Strategy GS Breakdown

Structuring an Answer (IBC Framework)

UPSC evaluators check hundreds of sheets. Make your answers easy to read, structured, and visually engaging. Follow the Introduction, Body, Conclusion (IBC) model:

  • Introduction (10-15% of space) — Start with a definition, dynamic fact, current news context, or constitutional article. Keep it short and precise.
  • Body (70-80% of space) — Answer all parts of the question. Use clear subheadings. Write in points, use bullet lists, draw flowcharts, maps, and diagrams.
  • Conclusion (10-15% of space) — Summarize with a forward-looking, optimistic, and constructive note. Suggest path breaking government initiatives or committee recommendations (e.g., ARC-2 reports).

General Studies Paper 1–4 Focus Areas

Each General Studies paper represents different competencies. Focus on these main sections:

  • GS Paper 1 (Heritage, Geography & Society) — Memorize timeline summaries for Indian Art, Culture, Modern History, and World Geography mapping. Include social issues case studies.
  • GS Paper 2 (Governance, Constitution & IR) — Cite constitutional articles, supreme court rulings, and committee recommendations. Map bilateral links.
  • GS Paper 3 (Economy, Science & Environment) — Use graphs, economic metrics, NITI Aayog guidelines, and security charts. Focus on agricultural and environmental policies.
  • GS Paper 4 (Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude) — Use diagrams (concentric values), cite quotes from philosophers, write realistic case study solutions, and practice moral dilemmas.
