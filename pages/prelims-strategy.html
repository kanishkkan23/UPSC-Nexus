The 4-Pillar Strategy for Prelims

Cracking UPSC Prelims requires a balanced mix of knowledge and test-taking logic. Focus your preparation on these four core pillars:

  • Pillar 1: Base NCERTs (Months 1-3) — Build conceptual foundations in Geography, History, Economics, and Polity using NCERT books from Class 6 to 12.
  • Pillar 2: Standard Reference Revision (Months 4-8) — Multiple revisions of static materials (Polity by Laxmikanth, Modern History by Spectrum, Environment by Shankar IAS).
  • Pillar 3: Current Affairs Integration (Daily) — Read The Hindu or Indian Express, mapping daily news back to static syllabus topics. Focus on geography maps and international relations.
  • Pillar 4: Mock Tests & MCQ Drill (Last 3 Months) — Solve at least 30-40 full-length mock test papers. Analyze the incorrect answers to understand gaps in logic and facts.

Cracking the CSAT (Paper-II)

CSAT has become a major roadblock for many aspirants. Even though it is qualifying (requires 33%), it must not be taken lightly. Follow these pointers to secure your qualifying score:

  • Identify Strengths — Categorize topics into Quant, Logical Reasoning, and Reading Comprehension.
  • Solve 10 Years' PYQs — UPSC repeats logic patterns in comprehension and aptitude. Thoroughly practice PYQs from 2015 to 2025.
  • Time Management — Practice solving at least 30-40 questions within 1 hour. Do not get stuck on tough maths puzzles.
