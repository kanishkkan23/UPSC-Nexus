UPSC Preliminary Examination

The First Gateway to the Civil Services Examination

The Preliminary Examination is the first stage of the UPSC Civil Services Examination. It serves as a screening test to select candidates for the Main Examination. Success in this stage requires conceptual clarity, continuous practice, and smart preparation.

Start Learning Exam Pattern

Overview of UPSC Preliminary Examination

Understand the purpose, structure, and importance of the first stage of the Civil Services Examination.

Purpose

The Preliminary Examination is conducted to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination.

Number of Papers

Two Papers are conducted:
• General Studies Paper-I
• CSAT (Paper-II)

Nature

Paper-I determines qualification for Mains while Paper-II is qualifying with minimum 33% marks.

Negative Marking

One-third of the marks assigned to a question are deducted for every incorrect answer.

UPSC Prelims Exam Pattern

The Preliminary Examination consists of two objective-type papers. Paper-I determines your qualification for the Main Examination, while Paper-II (CSAT) is qualifying in nature.

General Studies - Paper I

Mode Offline (OMR Based)
Questions 100
Total Marks 200
Duration 2 Hours
Negative Marking Yes (1/3)
Nature Merit Ranking

CSAT - Paper II

Mode Offline (OMR Based)
Questions 80
Total Marks 200
Duration 2 Hours
Qualifying Marks 33%
Nature Qualifying

12-Month Preparation Roadmap

A structured preparation plan helps aspirants cover the syllabus systematically and revise effectively before the examination.

Months 1 – 3

Build your foundation with NCERT books and basic standard reference books. Develop the habit of reading newspapers and making concise notes.

Months 4 – 6

Complete the remaining syllabus, revise previously studied topics, and begin solving Previous Year Questions (PYQs).

Months 7 – 9

Practice sectional and full-length mock tests regularly. Analyze mistakes and strengthen weak areas.

Months 10 – 12

Focus on multiple revisions, current affairs, mock tests, and effective time management before the examination.

✅ Do's

  • Read a newspaper every day.
  • Revise your notes regularly.
  • Solve Previous Year Questions.
  • Attempt mock tests consistently.
  • Prepare short revision notes.
  • Maintain a healthy daily routine.

❌ Don'ts

  • Do not study too many books.
  • Avoid skipping revision.
  • Don't ignore CSAT preparation.
  • Avoid studying without a plan.
  • Don't depend only on current affairs.
  • Avoid unnecessary stress before the exam.
